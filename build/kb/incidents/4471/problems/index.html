Three problems, not one. The queued parser fix closes only the first.

One ticket per problem

  • parser row: #1 · Shared parser reads day-first dates month first. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix.
  • hint row, “no ticket of its own”: #2 · booking-v3 form hints MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. Same symptom, different code: the parser fix does not touch it.
  • The one-day moves under the table: #3 · Dates move by one day across time zones, not a month flip. The desk read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and filed them under parser by tenant. A month flip cannot move a date by one day (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”), so neither fix above closes them.

Each ticket gives the number of reports it covers, counted as the table above counts them. The parser ticket covers the whole parser row, the one-day moves included; their ids are listed once, on the one-day-move ticket.

How it hid

  • Reports merge on subject alone (decision 0001). All three problems produce the same subjects, so all of them landed in one ticket.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. It disagrees with the tenant’s cause on dozens of reports, so counting by tag gets the numbers wrong.
  • The ticket export pages overlap. Count each report id once, as the table does.

What to do

  • Track each problem on its own ticket. Close #4471 only when all three are fixed.
  • Re-read decision 0001: it was last reviewed in June 2024 and merges reports that are not the same bug. A new record should replace it; this page does not edit it.
